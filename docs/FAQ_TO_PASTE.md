# Zero-Click Content Machine

**Q: What exactly do I get?**

A: 10 copy-paste prompts that turn a single topic into a LinkedIn carousel, a Twitter thread, a newsletter and more — delivered instantly as Markdown, PDF and DOCX.

**Q: Do I need a paid ChatGPT plan?**

A: No. Every prompt is written to work on the free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

**Q: How fast do I get access?**

A: Immediately. The download links appear on this page the second you check out — there is no waiting and no email confirmation step.

**Q: Do I need any technical or writing skill?**

A: None. Paste a prompt into your AI tool and swap the bracketed placeholders for your topic. If you can copy and paste, you can use this.

**Q: Can I use the output for client work?**

A: Yes. Everything you generate is yours to use for your own business or for clients. The only limit is that you may not resell the pack itself.

**Q: What if it does not work for my niche?**

A: The prompts are niche-agnostic by design — you supply the topic. If it genuinely is not a fit, reply on the product page and we will make it right.
