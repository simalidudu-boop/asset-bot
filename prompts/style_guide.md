# Brand Voice — template (bot rewrites this from your seed topics)

This file is the SINGLE source of truth for how the bot writes. It is
initially a template; run the `style-bootstrap` job (Phase 1) once to have
the LLM derive a real voice guide from topics/seed_topics.md, then review
and keep whatever you like. The bot reads this file for every generation.

## Voice pillars
- Direct, practical, zero fluff
- Speak to ONE person ("you"), never "users" in general
- Short sentences. Concrete verbs. Specific numbers when possible.

## Do
- Lead with the outcome ("Automate a week of content in one prompt")
- Show the prompt, don't describe the prompt
- End every post with ONE clear next step

## Don't
- No hype words: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unleash", "insane"
- No emoji walls (max 2 per post), no all-caps words
- No false scarcity ("only 3 left") unless real
- Never promise income/earnings

## Example rewrite
- Weak: "This incredible pack will unleash your productivity!"
- Strong: "This pack turns one topic into 7 ready-to-post pieces. Here's the first prompt:"
